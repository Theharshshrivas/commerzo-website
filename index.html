About COMMERZO

A focused digital learning space for commerce students.

COMMERZO brings commerce learning resources together in a simple, organised experience. Students can explore subjects by board and class, open chapter-wise study material, practise MCQs, attempt tests and review their performance. The platform is designed to make study resources easier to discover and use.

Created by Mr.Harsh Mahesh Shrivas · Madhya Pradesh, India.

COMMERZO features

Learning tools and resources currently available on the platform.

📚 Subject dashboard

Browse MP Board and CBSE subjects for Classes 11 and 12 with board and class filters.

🗂️ Chapter-wise resources

Find handwritten notes, previous-year papers, marking schemes, sample papers, important questions and syllabus material where published.

✍️ MCQ practice

Practise available multiple-choice questions with answer feedback and explanations where provided.

📊 Chapter tests

Attempt individual or selected-chapter tests and view marks, percentage and answer review.

👤 Student access

Continue with Google or use guest access for supported study features. Google sign-in is required to submit reviews.

⭐ Student reviews

Share feedback with moderation before it appears publicly. You can edit or delete your own review.
